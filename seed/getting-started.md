# Getting started with the Knowledge Hub

Welcome! The Knowledge Hub is where the team keeps runbooks, guides, decisions and reference material.

## Finding things

- **Library** lists every document. Filter by tag or owner, or type in the filter box to match titles.
- **Search** (coming soon) searches inside the text of every document, including PDFs.

## Adding documents

1. Open the **Library** and choose **Upload**.
2. Pick a file: Markdown, plain text, CSV, JSON, HTML or PDF, up to the size limit your admin has set.
3. Give it a clear title and a few tags such as `onboarding`, `runbook` or `architecture`.

Each new document is processed in the background. Its status moves from *queued* to *ready* when its text has been extracted and indexed.

## Who can change what

Anyone signed in can read and download every document. Only the person who uploaded a document, or an admin, can edit its title and tags or delete it.
