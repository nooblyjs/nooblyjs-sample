INCIDENT RUNBOOK
================

Use this runbook when a production service is degraded or down.

1. DECLARE
   - Post in the incident channel: what is broken, since when, who is leading.
   - The incident lead owns communication; everyone else investigates.

2. STABILISE
   - Check /health/ready on every instance. A 503 means a critical dependency failed its live check.
   - Check the logging dashboard for error spikes and the queueing dashboard for growing queues.
   - Roll back the last release if the incident started within an hour of a deploy.

3. COMMUNICATE
   - Update the status page every 30 minutes, even when nothing has changed.

4. RESOLVE
   - Confirm recovery with the health probes and a manual check of the main user journey.

5. LEARN
   - Write a blameless post-incident review within five working days and add it to the Knowledge Hub with the tag "post-incident".
